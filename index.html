Data Protection & Privacy Notice

Issued in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (ODPC) • Effective November 2026

1. Data Controller Identity

BodaBoda Queen (operating bodabodaqueen.co.ke, Nairobi, Kenya) acts as the Data Controller for personal data collected during bundle purchases, M-Pesa Express checkouts, and rider loyalty participation.

2. Personal Data We Collect

  • Phone Numbers: Safaricom recipient and M-Pesa payer MSISDN numbers (`2547XXXXXXXX` / `2541XXXXXXXX`).
  • Payment & Fulfillment Metadata: M-Pesa receipt numbers, transaction timestamps, bundle SKUs, and delivery confirmation logs.
  • Loyalty & Referral Records: System-generated referral codes (`BBQ-XXXXXX`), earned/redeemed loyalty points, and network security metadata (IP address for anti-spam rate limiting).
  • What We Never Collect: We never ask for, view, or store your M-Pesa PIN or national ID number.

3. Lawful Basis & Purpose of Processing

Under Section 30 of the Data Protection Act, we process your data strictly for contractual performance (delivering paid Safaricom bundles and verifying M-Pesa callbacks) and legitimate security interests (preventing STK Push spam and resolving failed deliveries). We never sell or rent rider phone numbers to third-party marketers.

4. Data Sharing & Cross-Border Infrastructure

Data is shared solely with Safaricom PLC (via Daraja APIs and USSD fulfillment gateways) to process M-Pesa payments and credit bundles, and hosted on encrypted Cloudflare edge infrastructure with strict access controls.

5. Retention & Your Data Subject Rights

Transaction records are retained for statutory tax and M-Pesa reconciliation purposes. Under Kenyan law, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your loyalty profile by contacting our Data Protection contact via WhatsApp/Phone at 0700 000 000.
